Delete Account

If you want to delete your KYX Network account, follow the steps below:

Steps to delete your account:

  1. Open the KYX Network app
  2. Go to Profile / Settings
  3. Select "Delete Account"

If you are unable to delete your account from the app, you can request deletion by contacting us:

Email: kyxcryptocoin@gmail.com

Data Deletion

Once requested, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 days.
